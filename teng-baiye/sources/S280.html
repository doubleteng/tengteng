滕白也指畫第一集Teng Baiye’s Finger Paintings, First Collection

1937-01 · 滕白也著，白玉文藝社出版；实物摄影：芜湖万卷书屋

版权页记民国二十六年一月出版，定价大洋壹元。目录收二十一题，多附藏者。售书照片包括三张作品页。The colophon dates the volume to January 1937 and gives a price of one silver dollar. Its bilingual contents list 21 works, many with owners. Three plates appear in the surviving sale photographs.
