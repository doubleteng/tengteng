滕白也指画赠小友

访问原网页 ↗Visit original website ↗

S043 · 观复 / 新民晚报 · 2012-04-14，B12

据观复采访滕国俊，1978年秋他读到《解放日报》介绍滕白也，托报社转信，获邀登门。老师住上海老城厢，虽视力不好、手抖，仍在餐桌前示范竹石翠鸟，后来把一幅仿郑板桥的指画墨竹题款赠给他。该报将平反与返沪记为1978年。In Guanfu’s interview, Teng Guojun recalls reading about Baiye in Jiefang Daily in autumn 1978 and sending him a letter through the newspaper. Baiye invited him to his home in Shanghai’s old city, demonstrated finger painting at his dining table, and later gave him an ink bamboo after Zheng Xie. The article dates rehabilitation and his return to Shanghai to 1978.
