S165 · Yenching University, President’s Office · 1932-04-16

公报预告滕圭将于4月21日下午4时在 McBrier Hall 103 室以英语作《中国艺术中的表现主义》讲演，辅以幻灯。原文称为他的首次公开讲演，应按燕京校内语境理解。The bulletin schedules Teng Kuei to lecture in English on Expressionism in Chinese Art, illustrated with lantern slides, at 4 p.m. on 21 April in McBrier 103.
