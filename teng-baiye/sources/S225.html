S225 · The Art Digest · 1932-05-01

旧金山条目在德扬纪念博物馆5月展讯中列滕圭的石版画。同页伍斯特艺术博物馆列第四届国际版画展，展至5月15日，未另列参展人名。The San Francisco calendar lists Teng’s lithographs at the de Young Memorial Museum. A separate Worcester entry lists the fourth international print exhibition through 15 May without naming its exhibitors.
