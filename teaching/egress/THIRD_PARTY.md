## Barcelona Pavilion plan and study geometry

`examples/barcelona-plan-tomalt.png` is **Plan Barcelona Pavillon.png** by
**TomAlt** (2010), supplied unchanged. Source:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Plan_Barcelona_Pavillon.png

Used under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

The Barcelona-specific geometry and thumbnail derived from that plan in
`src/precedents.mjs` / `src/examples.mjs` are adaptations under the same license.
Changes: simplified editable spatial polygons, wall and pool geometry, additional
furniture symbols, study opening positions and approximate scale; hypothetical
capacity/path-width inputs and a study public-way endpoint. Attribution and
license travel with the model in its `precedent` metadata and PDF report.
The source author does not endorse these teaching modifications.

The original building is by Ludwig Mies van der Rohe and Lilly Reich, 1929;
reconstructed 1986. Building history and official drawing links:
https://miesbcn.com/the-pavilion/

## Villa Savoye study sources

The independently drafted schematic model is informed by the Centre des monuments
nationaux visitor guide (three-floor diagram, p. 2) and Fondation Le Corbusier.
The official drawings/photos are linked, not bundled or represented as measured
survey data. Room use, dimensions, elevations and clear widths are expressly
teaching assumptions. Links are retained in the model and printable report.
