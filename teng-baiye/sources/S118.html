S118 · 滕圭 / 《婦女雜誌》 · 1923-10

署名滕圭的白话短诗。写信者从落笔、写信写到焚信，最后转向未能见面的收信人。正文跨两页。A vernacular poem signed Teng Gui follows a writer who composes and burns a letter before addressing its unseen recipient. The final lines appear on the next page.
