Lan-Hui Hsieh · 1926届；1927婚讯

Lan-Hui接近Han-Hui。校友刊写姓Hsieh，另载有她本人的婚讯，未发现她使用侯姓。The given name resembles Han-Hui, but the alumni record uses Hsieh and names William Shen as her husband.
