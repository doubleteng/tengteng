S129 · The North-China Herald · 1934-01-31

上海艺术俱乐部的演讲报道记录滕圭对线条、用笔与留白的解释：画中的笔意可由看画人补足。报道说他已加入中央大学，并在沪江大学讲授艺术。Reporting a lecture at the Shanghai Art Club, the newspaper recounts Teng’s comparison of brush lines and his explanation of a viewer’s role in completing an image. It describes him as on the staff of Central University and giving an art course at the University of Shanghai.
