Correspondence concerning Kwei Dun’s resident fellowship at Harvard

访问原网页 ↗Visit original website ↗

S171 · Yenching University / Harvard-Yenching Institute；George H. Chase 办公室、J. Leighton Stuart 收发件 · 1929-07-19—07-24

7月19日转录司徒雷登电报，具名推荐 Kwei Dun；7月20日哈佛燕京学社信笺上的回函确认北平行政委员会已批准，学社执委会此前的条件已满足。7月24日函把结果回告司徒雷登。信中所许为在哈佛的住校研究员资助。A 19 July letter relays Stuart’s cable recommending Kwei Dun. The 20 July reply on Institute stationery confirms the Peking committee’s approval, satisfying the Executive Committee’s condition for offering a resident fellowship at Harvard. A 24 July letter relays that reply to Stuart.
