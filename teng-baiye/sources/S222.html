Letter from Harvard’s bursar to B. A. Garside concerning the second quarter of the Harvard–Yenching fellowships

S222 · W. C. Saeger, Bursar, Harvard University · 1931-10-16

哈佛大学财务长萨格致燕京大学司库加赛德的信称，Bodde 与「Mr. Kwei」均已领取第二季度研究员津贴，因此此次汇款未包括二人。此处姓名只作 Kwei，可与同档案1929年任命函及1932年4月关于滕圭的结算函对读；本函未给出他个人所得金额或所在地。Harvard’s bursar told Yenching’s treasurer that Bodde and “Mr. Kwei” had already received their second-quarter fellowship stipends. Read with the 1929 award and April 1932 balance letter, this supplies a payment checkpoint. His individual amount and location are not given.
