S075 · Jean-Christophe Ammann / Das Werk: Architektur und Kunst · 1966，卷53，期12

德文文章以 Teng Kuei 之名称滕，记述其讲授东亚绘画、书法、构图；将相识写作1923年。Ammann names Teng Kuei in discussing instruction in East Asian painting, calligraphy and composition. His 1923 meeting date is a retrospective account.
