S246 · 画册编辑组／中央文献出版社 · 1999-10

电子目录署名「滕白也」，列《富春江山水（指画）》。出版社确认此册收入周恩来、邓颖超所藏书画148幅。所见公开样页没有这幅指画，目录编号10也不能据作印本页码；与报载《富春江歌》的关系尚待核对。The digital contents list names Teng Baiye beside a finger-painted Fuchun River Landscape. The publisher dates the 148-work album to October 1999. Item 10 is a digital index number. The relevant plate has not been examined and its identity with Fuchun River Song is unresolved.
