滕白也 · Teng Baiye

滕白也乳名阿小狗滕白也乳名阿小狗

S324 · 福尔摩斯报；龙池 · 11月14日，年份未见

龙池把“阿小狗”解释为乡人对滕白也少年时活泼伶俐的称呼，并叙述奉贤高桥出身、族人资助与海外求学的故事。文章后半转向他的雕塑活动，称他曾为戴季陶造像，又受意大利驻沪总领事之请制作墨索里尼像。乳名和资助经过仍属本文记述；墨索里尼胸像另见1935年的英文报刊图版。Longchi explains “A-Xiaogou” as a village nickname for Teng’s lively, quick-witted childhood and tells a story of Gaoqiao origins, support from relatives and overseas study. The article then describes portraits of Dai Jitao and a Mussolini sculpture said to have been commissioned through the Italian consul in Shanghai. The nickname and family support remain the article’s account; a Mussolini bust also appears in an illustrated English-language newspaper feature from 1935.
