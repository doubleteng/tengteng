Office of the Secretary Records: Chinese student index and subject files

S253 · Oberlin College Archives · 约1885–1955 / 1926–1928

校务秘书保存约1885–1955年中国学生姓名索引和1926–28年中国学生专题卷。公开目录不列出卡片上的姓名。The Secretary’s files identify a Chinese student index and a separate 1926–28 subject file, but do not reproduce the students’ cards.
