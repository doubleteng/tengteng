This development converted a single-family site in Seattle’s Lowrise 2 zone into four rowhouses totaling more than 6,000 square feet. Subdivision, zoning, building layout, and construction coordination shaped the project’s development strategy.

As a partner at Ju Square Development LLC, I managed the development during 2017–2020 and coordinated with the architect, engineering consultants, city officials, contractors, vendors, and brokers. Vandervort Architects provided the architectural design.
