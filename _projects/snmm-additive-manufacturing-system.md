The Single-Nozzle Multi-Filament (SNMF) system blends multiple filament feeds through an actively mixed extrusion head. Programmable composition enables continuous material transitions within a printed object.

A numerical model describes the transition between material mixtures. Experimental validation links feed commands to the resulting material distribution, supporting more precise placement of material interfaces.

Image sampling, two-dimensional discrete patches, and three-dimensional surface division provide three ways to specify multimaterial objects. Six case studies demonstrate the fabrication of complex geometries with different functional requirements.
