S132 · The North-China Herald · 1935-09-25

上海报纸预告Shiao Chao Chinese Music Research Institute 十周年音乐会，把 Teng Kuei 列为筹办首演的顾问委员会成员之一。原文未列他为演奏者。A notice for the Shiao Chao Chinese Music Research Institute’s anniversary concert lists Teng Kuei on the advisory committee for the event planned at the Lyceum Theatre. The notice assigns him no performance.
