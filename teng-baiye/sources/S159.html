S159 · The Art Institute of Chicago · 1930

馆方1930年报的临时展览记录列第二届国际石版画与木刻展。它可核对芝加哥展览的届次和年份，但本次取得的年报页没有逐件作品清单。The annual report lists the second international exhibition of lithography and wood engraving under 1930. The report documents the edition, without supplying a checklist that identifies Rocky Landscape.
