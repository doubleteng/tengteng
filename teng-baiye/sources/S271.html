Willard Beard to Kathleen Beard, 8 August 1926

S271 · Willard L. Beard／Beard family papers · 1926-08-08

父亲写信告诉女儿，Mr. Nga Geng Guong刚离开福州，赴欧柏林读书。编者附的毕业班照片将他列作福州籍教育专业。他是实际在该校就读的福建男士。Beard told his daughter in August 1926 that Nga Geng Guong had just left Foochow to study at Oberlin. The accompanying yearbook photograph identifies him as an education student.
