S073 · The Cleveland Museum of Art · 1948–1949，印刷页65，编号449

原目录列 TENG, KWEI 及第449号 Rain on the Yangtse，归 Lewis B. Williams 夫妇收藏。履历记生于1902年苏州，与其他生平来源有分歧。Printed p. 65, entry 449, lists Rain on the Yangtse in the Williams collection. The biographical entry gives 1902 and Suzhou; this is preserved as the catalogue’s claim.
