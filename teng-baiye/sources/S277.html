大同大學第四十八期同學錄

S277 · 大同大學 · 1935年12月

印页66记另一户在“上海康梯路呂班路口光裕坊二號”。对照1934年《申报》所记夫妇婚宅“光裕坊六号”，可将住宅定位于今建国东路、重庆南路路口附近。Page 66 locates another household, No. 2 Guangyu Fang, at Kangti and Lüban roads. Read alongside Shen Bao’s report identifying No. 6 as the couple’s home, it places the lane near today’s Jianguo East Road and Chongqing South Road.
