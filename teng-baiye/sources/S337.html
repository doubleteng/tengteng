司徒雷登与卢卡斯关于南京陆军顾问团的通信Stuart–Lucas correspondence on the Army Advisory Group, June–July 1947

S337 · U.S. Department of State, Office of the Historian · 1947-06-05 / 06-28 / 07-07

FRUS 1947年第VII卷第671号文件（893.20 Mission/7–747；第863号公文）收录司徒雷登6月5日向卢卡斯询问顾问团工作、卢卡斯6月28日答复及7月7日使馆转呈的公文。通信直接证明滕白也年表所记入职月份，陆军顾问团正在南京开展工作。FRUS 1947, volume VII, document 671 (893.20 Mission/7–747; despatch 863) contains Stuart’s inquiry of 5 June, Lucas’s reply of 28 June and the Embassy’s covering despatch of 7 July. It directly confirms the AAG’s activity in Nanking during Teng’s reported month of entry.

与生平记载的关系Relation to the biographical record

正文没有滕白也或俞大纲姓名。卢卡斯回复所附五项报告均未收入FRUS，主题包括活动地点与美方人员数量、训练、器材、费用和未来资金。档号指向完整公文及附件，但不能把附件称为中国雇员名册。The text names neither Teng nor Yu Ta-kang. Five annexes to Lucas’s reply were not printed; they concern activities and U.S. personnel numbers, training, equipment, costs and future funding. The file number points to the full despatch, not a demonstrated Chinese employee roster.
