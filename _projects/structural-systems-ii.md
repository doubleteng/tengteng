## Force, form, and construction

Structural Systems II develops architectural judgment through five structural families: form-active, vector-active, section-active, surface-active, and height-active systems. Students trace how loads are received, transferred, and discharged, then use that understanding to organize material and space.

The course moves repeatedly between analysis, transformation, construction, and evaluation. Sketches and calculations establish a structural proposition; digital models and technical drawings define its geometry; physical assembly and loading expose the consequences of connections, tolerances, and material choices.
