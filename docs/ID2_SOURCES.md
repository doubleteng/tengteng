## Review and selection

All 19 supplied files were reviewed: 16 PDFs, the method comparison document, and two full-resolution PNG boards. This includes the complete 85-page instructor lecture (not only the 23-page mini lecture), the eight-page Salatyan technical drawing set, all student presentation/model PDFs, both Zygadlo boards, and the two videos embedded in Zygadlo’s submission.

The revised page uses the full title `Integrated Design Studio II` in both the project heading and preview cards. Juan Pineros’s architectural visualization and Dylan Simmons’s sectional perspective replace the two physical model photographs at the opening. The thumbnail uses Juan’s architectural visualization.

Selection follows the instructor’s teaching requirements: urban forces and user scenarios; sectional continuity; legible entrances and returns; move/pause sequences; selective visibility and controlled artist access; shared voids; and coordination with program, structure, daylight, and enclosure. Project descriptions are curatorial readings of the supplied work, not student grades or claims that every proposal completely resolves each requirement.

The syllabus and brief identify light, material, interior space, drawing conventions, iteration, and representation as course concerns. Instructor lecture pages 76–84 sharpen the behavioral criteria: differentiated users, a visible entrance, alternating movement and pause, selective studio visibility, seating outside the main flow, voids touched at selected levels, and access that can respond to changing city rhythms.

Dylan Simmons explicitly credits ChatGPT/OpenAI image generation based on his original design on presentation pages 1 and 21. That attribution is retained in the public caption and individual image descriptions. The syllabus permits generative AI use with acknowledgement. These renderings supplement his architectural drawings and are not described as built photography.

Mateusz Zygadlo’s pages 21–22 contain RichMedia video objects, not missing illustrations. The original H.264 MP4 streams were extracted unchanged; the two poster frames come from those videos. The interior stills are described as spatial/model studies without asserting an unverified physical medium.

Repeated progressive program diagrams, redundant presentation-board copies, out-of-focus photographs, and repetitive near-identical model views are not repeated on the page. The complete supplied archive remains linked. Source PDFs are not duplicated in the website repository.

The page uses existing project/media blocks and remains editable through the content studio. Teaching catalogue membership stays in `_data/portfolio.yml`; homepage and institution listings derive from the project collection. No shared page templates were changed.
