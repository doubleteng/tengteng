宾大原藏总目录把 T’eng, Kuei 放在一般通信第46盒的起首，合并记为文件夹1698–1714，末项为 Time。据此可向馆方申请原信。Penn’s inventory begins one range of correspondents with T’eng, Kuei and ends it with Time. The grouped entry is in box 46, folders 1698–1714; this is a location range, not a letter count.
