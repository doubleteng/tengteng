Yenching University Bulletin: College of Arts and Letters, 1932–1933

S173 · Yenching University, College of Arts and Letters · 1932-08

教师名册将 Teng Kuei 列作美术讲师，系属国文学系。课程页以 Mr. T’eng 列出五门：中国艺术鉴赏、中国艺术技法、中国雕塑入门、室内装饰、比较艺术。雕塑课要求塑造及雕刻实作，可实察北平雕像和纪念物。The roster lists Teng Kuei as Lecturer in Fine Arts under Chinese. Five courses carry his name, in art appreciation, Chinese masters’ techniques, sculpture, interior decoration and comparative art. The sculpture syllabus required modeling and carving and allowed for field study of monuments around Peiping.
