评论指出 Kwei Teng 的 Weyhe 画廊展于10月26日闭幕。作者对指画技法本身持保留意见，与同期其他评论的评价有别。Pemberton confirms the closing date of the Weyhe exhibition and questions how much the use of fingers matters to the viewer. His response provides a counterpoint to other contemporary reviews.
