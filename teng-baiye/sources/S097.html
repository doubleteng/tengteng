S097 · The Harvard Crimson · 1930-04-30

波士顿二十世纪俱乐部展出滕圭的40幅绘画与素描，预计展至5月16日。报道指名《Tranquillity》，并称画的是女神。The Twentieth Century Club in Boston was showing 40 of Teng’s paintings and drawings through May 16. The reporter identified Tranquillity as a study of a goddess.
