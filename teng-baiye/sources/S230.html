S230 · Brooklyn Children’s Museum Archives · 馆藏1881–2011；馆方检索指南

布鲁克林儿童博物馆把1930至1931年《Children’s Museum News》存于第85盒第5夹；第16盒另有立体场景往来信和入藏资料。这些卷宗可用于追查滕圭与Franklin制作的马可·波罗场景。The museum’s finding aid locates its 1930–31 newsletter run in box 85, folder 5, and diorama files in box 16. These are routes to the March 1930 reference in Clarke; the finding aid does not itself credit an individual maker.
