Pinbed investigates an adjustable printing surface for depositing material along curved geometries. A 6 × 6 array of linear actuators supports flexible metal strips and a cast silicone surface. The prototype provides an approximately 30 × 30-inch working area with six inches of vertical travel.

A Grasshopper controller samples a target surface into height values and sends commands to three control modules. Hall-effect sensors track motor rotation to determine actuator travel. The same digital surface provides a reference for the robot’s printing toolpath.

I designed and assembled the mechanical system, control boards, and software at Cornell University. Initial printing tests explored the use of the reconfigurable bed for nonplanar fabrication.
