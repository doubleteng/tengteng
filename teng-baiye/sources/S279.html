介紹美術家滕白也Introducing the artist Teng Baiye

1933-11-05 · 《申报》

第4版左下广告列铜像、油像、笔画、指画润例，附介绍人名单。指画每方尺十四元，笔画十元，油像二百元。The lower left of page 4 lists commissions and sponsors. Per square chi, the notice asks fourteen yuan for a finger painting, ten for a brush painting and two hundred for an oil portrait.
