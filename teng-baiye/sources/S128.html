The Shanghai Art Club “At Home” and Other Events

S128 · The North-China Herald · 1934-01-17

上海艺术俱乐部介绍新画室和课程，称滕圭将教授雕塑与中国画，并预告一场关于中国艺术表现主义的讲演。报道还称他正在上海授课。The Shanghai Art Club announces that Teng Kwei will teach sculpture and Chinese painting and give a talk on expressionism. This is a programme announcement.
