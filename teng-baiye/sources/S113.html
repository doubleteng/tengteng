Mark Tobey: poeticamente astratto / Poetically Abstract

S113 · Dominique Stella、Philippe Daverio 编 / Agnellini Arte Moderna, Brescia；SHIN production · 2009

布雷西亚展览图录分别在策展文章及年表中记载滕圭教授托贝笔法、书法和构图。文章写1923年，年表作1923/24，应保留两处表述的差异。Dominique Stella’s essay places the meeting in 1923. The catalogue chronology uses 1923/24 and identifies brush technique, calligraphy and composition among Teng’s teachings.
