Yintai Centre combines retail, residential accommodation, offices, a hotel, and serviced apartments in Chengdu. The design work coordinates the podium with several towers and their distinct program requirements.

As a design technologist, I contributed to master planning, podium design, hotel and office façades, environmental analysis, construction-document coordination, and 3D model management. Parametric models supported the comparison of elevation patterns with solar conditions and client requirements.

During design development, I studied six spandrel-glass options and developed parapet and mechanical-room façade solutions.
