S196 · 上海《申報》 · 1934-04-20

报道区分了在沪已完成的朱葆三遗像、姚慕莲肖像，以及新接受的吴市长肖像委托。吴市长一件尚待开工。The notice distinguishes two completed portraits, of Zhu Baosan and Yao Mulian, from a new commission for Mayor Wu. No completion date is given for the mayor’s portrait.
