S135 · University of Washington / University of Washington Press · 1925

1924–25学年的学生名册将他登记为 Duncan, Benjamin (Dun, Kwei)，家乡栏为 China。同一条目将中文名的罗马拼写和全英文姓名对应起来。The 1924–25 student register lists Duncan, Benjamin (Dun, Kwei), from China, directly linking the English name Benjamin Duncan with Kwei Dun.
