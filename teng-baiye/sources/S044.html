记滕国俊在20世纪70年代结识上海文史馆馆员滕白也，并由此从兼习多种画法转向专攻指画。文中所列《钟山风雨》等均为滕国俊作品。A 2018 feature recalls that meeting Baiye in the 1970s led Guojun to specialize in finger painting. Works such as Zhongshan Storms discussed in the feature are by Guojun.
