Loo Y. How · 1927–1932

欧伯林名录原印Loo Y. How。船单中拼作How Loo Ye的女子被考释为上海夏家的夏璐懿，但1927年该船单记她拟赴佐治亚州Wesleyan College。未见转学记录或中英文双名学籍。The Oberlin directory names Loo Y. How. An arrival-record study identifies How Loo Ye as Xia Luyi, but gives her 1927 destination as Wesleyan College, Georgia. Their identities have not been directly linked by an enrollment or transfer record.

1929手册印40；1932年鉴印140

HOW, Loo Y. (Miss) · 1929

印本只给英文名。夏家船单提示可能的对名；尚缺从Wesleyan到Oberlin的学籍关联，不能把她认作侯。Name, field and address transcribed as printed.
