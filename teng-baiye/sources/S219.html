West Bund Art & Design Fair — Shanghai

S219 · Galerie Jeanne Bucher Jaeger, Paris · 2019-11-07—10

法国画廊首次参加上海西岸艺博会时展出托贝和杨诘苍作品，托贝条以 Teng Kwei 具名回溯书法学习。资料所列1954至1970年作品属于托贝。The Paris gallery’s Shanghai presentation paired Mark Tobey and Yang Jiechang. Its Tobey entry names Teng Kwei as his calligraphy teacher; the ten works dated 1954–1970 were by Tobey.
