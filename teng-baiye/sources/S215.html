S215 · Marguerite Hui Müller-Yao / Rheinische Friedrich-Wilhelms-Universität Bonn；Verlag der Buchhandlung Walther König · 1985

作者把托贝记述的笔法课与他讨论如何用线、体量和光描绘树木并读，又分析滕对西方绘画如“有框的洞”的评论。Müller-Yao compares Tobey’s report of brush lessons with Teng to his discussion of line, mass and light in depicting trees. She also examines the framed-hole critique attributed to Teng.
