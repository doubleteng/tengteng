Tobey, Mark (1890–1976)

S194 · Deloris Tarzan Ament / HistoryLink.org · 2003-02-16

西雅图艺术评论家 Ament 记述滕向托贝介绍中国笔法。其叙述将1934年勒奇与托贝同行的欧洲路段、两人分开后托贝在上海拜访滕的行程分开。Ament recounts Teng’s introduction of Chinese brushwork to Tobey and distinguishes Tobey’s 1934 travel with Bernard Leach from his visit to Teng in Shanghai.
