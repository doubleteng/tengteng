Guide to the Records of the Departments of European, American, and Contemporary Art: 1929 Summer Show

S105 · Brooklyn Museum Archives · 馆方目录；展览1929年

目录载展期6月1日至10月1日。两份展览档案包括参展者姓名地址、目录、画作价格表及收据。它们可用于追查滕参展作品；目录未列其逐件题名。Two files for the June–October 1929 summer exhibition contain artist addresses, catalogues, an exhibitor list, painting prices and receipts. The guide does not enumerate Teng’s individual works.
