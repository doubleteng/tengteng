Harvard Man Runs Refugee Factory

访问原网页 ↗Visit original website ↗

S233 · China Information Committee, Hankow / China at War · 1938-06

1938年6月在汉口出版的英文通讯说，雕塑家Teng Kwei当时在汉口组织数百名难民制作前线军需品，包括袜、扇、草鞋和毛巾。主要工人为妇女和少女，工场设在两处原校舍，项目隶属新生活运动组织。The Hankow bulletin reported that Teng was supervising hundreds of refugee workers making socks, fans, straw sandals and towels for troops. Mostly women and teenage girls, they worked in two converted schools under a New Life Movement Association project.
