## What makes a worked egress case ready

- Identify the building, construction or alteration scope, authority, adopted IBC edition, dated approval and any applicable alternate means. Document each source sheet and revision. A bid drawing alone does not establish approval.
- Select a manageable part of the plan. Its exit route must continue through every intervening level to a documented exterior discharge/public way. Include people entering shared corridors, stairs or exits from outside the selected area.
- Record a reference calculation for each included room: use, area, net/gross or fixed seating, load factor and occupant load. Match the source widths and distances; mark missing information rather than inventing it. Source dimensions take priority over an image-derived measurement.
- Reconcile the baseline with the actual drawings before it enters the student gallery. Regression checks should compare specific loads, capacities, distances and complete connections to the source. If the app's IBC 2021 rules differ from the adopted code, label those results as a separate classroom comparison.
- Students edit a copy. Results describe their revision, not the original building's permit. Published materials require suitable reuse rights; link to the owner's drawings when republication is not authorized.
