滕白也 · Teng Baiye

滕白也之過去情史滕白也之過去情史

S325 · 金钢钻；娑娑 · 1936年7月8日

娑娑以滕白也往来京沪、承造孙中山铜像的近况引出一段“过去情史”。文章声称，他任教时曾与一位姓郭的女学生相恋，并见过其经营公司的父亲；一次宴席上的中西礼俗冲突，最终使婚事告吹。作者用饭桌动作和人物对白制造戏剧性，未提供郭女士全名或当事人的证言。Suosuo begins with Teng’s travels between Nanjing and Shanghai and his Sun Yat-sen statue commission, then recounts an alleged earlier romance. The story claims that he fell in love with a student surnamed Guo and met her businessman father, but that a clash over dining customs ended the proposed marriage. Dramatic gestures and dialogue drive the narrative; neither Guo’s full name nor testimony from the people involved is supplied. It records the popular press’s storytelling about the artist’s private life.
