S267 · Chinese Students Alliance in U.S.A.；各年编者见卷首 · 1929–1933

逐年核录欧伯林姓名、所学或通讯地址。1929年版分列学音乐的Miss Nyoh Tsung Chang和音乐学院的Miss Loo Y. How。通讯地址或夫人称谓未作在读凭证。The US Chinese student handbooks list names, fields and addresses. The 1929 volume has separate entries for Nyoh Tsung Chang (music) and Loo Y. How (Conservatory of Music). A local address or a married title alone does not prove enrollment.
