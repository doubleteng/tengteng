S240 · 吴霖／《北京晚报·五色土》 · 2023-09-26

吴霖据《顾颉刚日记》记述，1932年10月2日洪业在北平设家宴，滕白也与顾颉刚、马鉴、张铨、盛成等同席。这段材料记录燕京时期的私人往来。Drawing on Gu Jiegang’s diary, Wu reports that Teng Baiye attended a dinner at Hong Ye’s home in Beiping on 2 October 1932 with Gu Jiegang, Ma Jian, Zhang Quan and Sheng Cheng. Wu does not identify Teng in the wedding photograph featured in the article.
