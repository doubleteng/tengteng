S156 · Rijksmuseum，Amsterdam · 1931；赠入1973

馆藏纸本石版画。作者记作 K’uei Teng，年代1931，尺寸高×宽404 × 289毫米。1973年由 C. Zigrosser 赠入。纸缘有英文旧题 Two Birds。The Rijksmuseum attributes this lithograph to K’uei Teng, dates it to 1931 and measures it at 404 × 289 mm (height × width). C. Zigrosser gave it in 1973. A lower-margin inscription reads Two Birds.
