S161 · T’eng, Kuei／University of Pennsylvania, Kislak Center · 1928–1952及未标日期

滕氏通信档案共152件、584叶，包括家人照片、剪报、书稿及1931–32年欧洲行程表。馆方提要涉及其在英国与Leon Underwood、在法国借住Warneke夫妇工作室、经印度返华，以及1932–52年在华通信中的教学、难民救济和赴美谋职意愿。The collection contains 152 items (584 leaves), including letters, family photographs, book texts and a European itinerary. Penn’s description traces the 1931–32 tour through England, France, Spain, Germany and Italy, then home via India. Later letters discuss teaching, refugee aid, wartime separation, and the possibility of work in the United States in 1952.
