日內瓦中國繪畫展覽

S204 · 上海《申報》 · 1934-09-21

报载日内瓦中国绘画展览所列画家中有滕白也。该条可与欧洲展览资料互查，未列他参展作品题名。Teng’s name appears in the Shanghai newspaper’s list of artists associated with the Geneva exhibition of Chinese painting. It does not identify his exhibits.
