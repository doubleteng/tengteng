S237 · William C. Seitz / Archives of American Art, Smithsonian Institution；Jayna Hanson 编目 · 访谈1962；档案编目2011

史密森尼说明，赛茨生前将托贝访谈转录交给研究生丽贝卡·马西·莱恩，1988年由她捐入档案馆。克拉克（S036注37）引用转录第41页论树木、感知与线条，并与滕圭在上海所讲的画树方法对读。The Smithsonian identifies the provenance of the interview transcript: Seitz gave it to graduate student Rebecca Massie Lane, who donated it to the Archives in 1988. Clarke cites transcript p. 41 alongside Teng’s remarks in Shanghai about painting a tree.
