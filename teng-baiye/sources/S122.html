談佛教藝術

S122 · 滕白也 / 廣西省佛教會《獅子吼月刊》 · 1941-03-15

滕白也写于“佛教與美術特輯”，以图表区分佛教造像类型，继而谈石刻、金铜造像与绘画，也记述巨赞约稿。正文署滕白也。Writing for a special issue on Buddhism and art, Teng diagrams types of Buddhist imagery and discusses stone and bronze sculpture and painting. He mentions Juzan’s invitation to contribute.
