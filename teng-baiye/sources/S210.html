滕白也對市立博物館貢獻意見

S210 · 上海《申報》 · 1935-07-05

报纸逐条刊出滕白也对筹设中的上海市立博物馆的建议，包括征集中国文物、以复制品讲授西洋艺术、向私人借展和按专题组织展览；亦记载他提出迁移敦煌壁画的主张。The newspaper set out Teng’s proposals for the projected city museum: collecting Chinese works, teaching Western art through reproductions, borrowing from private collectors and organising thematic exhibitions. It also records his proposal to move Dunhuang murals.
