S166 · Yenching University, President’s Office；Teng Kuei（具名倡议） · 1932-12-10

燕京大学预告12月12日滕圭与 Clara C. Park 在 Wei Hsin Yuan 新校画廊合展绘画与素描。同一期刊出滕圭本人的校园艺术社倡议，提议设常设画廊、邀请北京美术机构合作，每年办12至15场展览。The bulletin announces a 12 December joint exhibition by Clara C. Park and Teng at the new gallery in Wei Hsin Yuan. In a signed proposal, Teng advocates a campus art club and a permanent gallery, with 12 to 15 exhibitions a year in cooperation with the Peking Institute of Fine Arts.
