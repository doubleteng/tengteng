Catalog of Abilene Christian College: degrees and the School of Fine Arts

S276 · Abilene Christian College；Abilene Christian University Library／UNT · 1930–31、1933–34、1935–36

1933–34年校目列学校颁授B.A.、B.S.，两项主修表均未列音乐。音乐课可计入学士学分，另载钢琴、声乐、小提琴证书条件。德州这所学校当时确实教音乐，校目中未见两类专名音乐学士或侯氏个人记录。1935–36年校目印页69则明确可主修音乐取得文学士，晚于1934年婚讯。Abilene listed B.A. and B.S. degrees. Music did not appear among the majors in the 1933–34 catalogue. Music study could earn credit toward a degree, and the fine arts school offered certificates, including violin. Neither catalogue identifies Hou personally. The 1935–36 catalogue, published after the November 1934 engagement notice, explicitly lists music as a B.A. major on page 69.
