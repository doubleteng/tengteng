S013 · 上海市文史研究馆 · 印刷页170

馆方记他解放后任上海医学模型厂雕塑技师，1958年被划为右派、1979年平反。馆员档案配有肖像，未注明摄制日期。The institute identifies him as a sculptural model technician at the Shanghai Medical Model Factory and dates the rightist designation to 1958 and rehabilitation to 1979. Its member portrait carries no photographic date.
