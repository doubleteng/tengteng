The Oriental Influence in the Work of Mark Tobey

S116 · Julie Marie McDonnell / National College of Art and Design, Dublin · 1995

都柏林艺术学院论文转述托贝向滕学习毛笔的回忆，并介绍1934年上海重聚时接触当地艺术生活的经过。文内名写 Teng Keui。McDonnell quotes Tobey’s account of a brush lesson and discusses their reunion in Shanghai in 1934. The thesis uses the spelling Teng Keui.
