家政科報名踴躍

S199 · 上海《申報》 · 1934-06-26

中华妇女节制会和沪大商学院合办家政科的招生消息，将滕白也列为「家庭美术」课程的受聘教师。A recruitment notice for a domestic-science programme jointly organised by the women’s temperance association and the University of Shanghai business school names Teng as its instructor in art for the home.
