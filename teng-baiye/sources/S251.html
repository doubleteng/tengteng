侯涵辉的求学、相识与家存小提琴

S251 · 家人提供 · 2026-11-03 记录

家人确认侯涵辉曾在美国学习音乐，回国后在上海与滕白也相识。家中至今保存一把当时留下的小提琴。家人另述她于1995年逝世，遗体在上海捐献。Her family recalls that Hou Hanhui studied music in the United States and met Teng Baiye in Shanghai after returning. The family still has a violin from that time. They give 1995 as her year of death and describe a body donation in Shanghai.
