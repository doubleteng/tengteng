巴黎中國畫展經過（三）

S244 · 上海《申報》 · 1933-11-29

报上巴黎中国画展记事将滕白也列入七十一位出品画家之中。未列他的作品题名。The third instalment of this account of the Paris Chinese painting exhibition names Teng among its 71 contributing artists. His paintings are not identified by title.
