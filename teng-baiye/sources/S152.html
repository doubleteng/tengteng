S152 · Art Gallery of Toronto，今 Art Gallery of Ontario · 1932-10

多伦多美术馆花卉与静物展的第87号为 Teng-Kwei《Spring Blossoms》，由纽约 E. Weyhe 借展，列价10.00。目录将其国籍误作 Japanese。该组包含版画、素描与石版画，条目没有单列技法。Entry 87 lists Teng-Kwei’s Spring Blossoms, lent by E. Weyhe and priced at 10.00. The printed catalogue misidentifies him as Japanese. Its combined prints, drawings and lithographs heading does not specify this work’s medium.
