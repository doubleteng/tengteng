This research integrates thermal insulation into the material organization of masonry components through gradient multimaterial additive manufacturing. A single-nozzle process enables transitions between material mixtures within each printed unit.

The wall-system studies place reinforced material in regions with higher stress demand and insulating material in lower-stress regions. Print-quality and functional tests examine the compatibility of the material combinations and their structural and thermal behavior.

The work received the Best Paper Runner-Up Award at ACADIA 2024.
