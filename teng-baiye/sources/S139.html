Tyee 1926 Yearbook: Chinese Students’ Club

S139 · Associated Students of the University of Washington · 1926

校内中国学生会列 Kwei Dun 为国际青年会代表，会员表也记其名。照片题注中的 Dunn 与会员表中的 Yenchuen Y. Dunn 拼写相同；该页不能据此认定滕的肖像。The Chinese Students’ Club lists Kwei Dun as its representative to the International Y.M.C.A. and also names him among its members. A separate member, Yenchuen Y. Dunn, is listed on the same page. The photograph caption does not identify Kwei Dun.
