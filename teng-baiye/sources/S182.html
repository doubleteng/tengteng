An International

S182 · The Art Digest · 1931-12-15

费城版画会第四届国际蚀刻、干刻与木版画展的名单在中国项下列 Teng Kwei。展至1932年1月2日，报道预告随后到布鲁克林博物馆和伍斯特博物馆展出，未注明滕圭作品的题名和技法。The Philadelphia Print Club’s fourth international exhibition of etchings, drypoints and wood blocks lists Teng Kwei under China. The report gives 2 January as the closing date and announces a subsequent tour to Brooklyn and Worcester. His title and medium are unspecified.
