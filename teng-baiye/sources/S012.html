S012 · 包铭新、王瑞华（《滕白也研究》）；东华大学出版社 · 2008年3月，第1版

包铭新、王瑞华著《滕白也研究》。卷首有三幅肖像；印页1–60为生平与创作，61–70为年表，71–104为三篇文章，105–130为绘画和雕塑附录，131–134为参考文献和后记。Study of Teng Baiye by Bao Mingxin and Wang Ruihua includes portraits, a biography, chronology, three writings, illustrated works, references and an afterword. The second study in the volume concerns two other artists.
