S092 · Nicolas Idier / Perspectives chinoises；英译 N. Jayaram · 2014年第1期

书评将 Teng Kroei、Teng Quay 等罕见拼法与滕圭联系起来，并讨论雕塑、指画与托贝在上海的交往。拼法源于二手研究，不能单据音近认定同一人。Idier discusses the spellings Teng Kroei and Teng Quay alongside better-known variants and compares Teng’s sculpture and finger painting with Tobey’s practice. These variants provide search terms, not independent identification.
