S074 · William C. Seitz / Museum of Modern Art, New York · 1962

印刷页47及49讨论滕圭与托贝的用线交流和托贝在上海滕家居住的经历。年表将初识写为1923–24。Seitz discusses line and form in the artists’ exchanges and records Tobey staying with Teng’s family in Shanghai. The chronology uses 1923–24 for their meeting.
