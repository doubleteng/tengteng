馆藏纸本石版画。作者记作 K’uei Teng，年代ca. 1930，尺寸高×宽287 × 400毫米。1973年由 C. Zigrosser 赠入。纸缘有英文旧题 Willow Landscape。The Rijksmuseum attributes this lithograph to K’uei Teng, dates it to ca. 1930 and measures it at 287 × 400 mm (height × width). C. Zigrosser gave it in 1973. A lower-margin inscription reads Willow Landscape.
