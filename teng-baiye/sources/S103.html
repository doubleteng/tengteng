S103 · University of Washington Libraries, Special Collections · 1989年整理；照片未标年

托贝遗藏照片的目录在第2箱列有“Teng Kuei and family”，计8张。该批资料于1988年由西雅图艺术博物馆转交。此处未列各照片日期或个人姓名。Box 2 lists eight photographs of Teng Kuei and family. The Seattle Art Museum transferred this group of Tobey estate photographs in 1988. The inventory does not date or individually identify the eight photographs.
