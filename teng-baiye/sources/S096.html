Washington State Library Historic Structure Report — 5.1 Profiles: Mark Tobey

S096 · Washington State Department of General Administration · 2002年8月

州立图书馆建筑报告在介绍托贝的公共艺术时提及滕圭的书法教学与上海重访。传略把初识写为1923年，不能用来覆盖1924年入境记录。A Washington State Library report traces Tobey’s calligraphy studies to Teng and recounts the Shanghai visit. Its 1923 first-meeting date is retrospective and differs from the documented December 1924 arrival.
