El oriente de Chillida

S112 · Paloma Galante Rodero / Espacio, Tiempo y Forma. Serie VII, Historia del Arte, UNED · 2011

作者以滕圭向托贝传授书法为例，与雕塑家奇利达学习东方艺术的方式相比较。文中两次具名 Teng K’uei，未称滕与奇利达直接见面。The author compares Tobey’s personal instruction from Teng K’uei with Eduardo Chillida’s different route to East Asian art. Page 358 names Teng twice and supplies no evidence of contact between Teng and Chillida.
