I developed this boot camp and taught it six times between 2021 and 2024. Students construct a desktop six-axis robot and develop a Grasshopper-based controller before applying the system to small fabrication tasks.

The course connects robot geometry and kinematics with mechanical assemblies, circuit design, motor control, and end-effector development. Participants use the assembled machines for applications such as foam cutting and material handling.

Lectures and remote studio sessions accompany the hardware and software work. The course begins with prior knowledge of Rhino and Grasshopper and introduces the programming needed to control the robot.
