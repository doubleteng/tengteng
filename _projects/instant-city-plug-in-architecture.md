Instant City proposes the adaptive reuse of the former Chongqing Iron and Steel Company district. The project retains industrial buildings and railway infrastructure as the framework for new civic, commercial, and residential uses.

Prefabricated modules travel along the existing rail network and plug into large-span factory buildings. Structural grids organize their placement, allowing interior arrangements to change with program and demand. The proposal also adapts this module system to residential frameworks on the site’s hillsides.

Developed as an undergraduate thesis with Kangyi Zhen during 2011–2012, the project connects industrial heritage with a strategy for incremental urban change.
