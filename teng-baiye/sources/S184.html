S184 · La Chine illustrée / Bibliothèque Sino-Internationale, Genève · 1936，第11期

滕白也站在孙中山胸像左后方。法、英、德三种图注都将作品归给 Teng Peh-yeh。法、英文称材质为大理石。构图与中央社所藏国际社照片0002相同，画报保留了这张照片在日内瓦的刊载记录。Teng stands behind his portrait bust of Sun Yat-sen. The French, English and German captions name him as Teng Peh-yeh. The French and English call the bust marble. The composition matches agency photograph 0002 in Taiwan’s Central News Agency collection.
