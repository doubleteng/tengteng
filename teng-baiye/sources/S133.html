Specimens from Prof. Teng Kuei’s Studio

S133 · The North-China Herald · 1935-11-20

原版十图分别题作孔子、墨索里尼胸像、孔祥熙浮雕、孙中山、Shu Long-si、南京孙中山纪念模型、朱葆三、跑狗场奖杯、上海板球俱乐部奖杯，以及 Mrs. Daystar Hon Teng 与其塑像合影。A full-page feature captions ten studio images, among them portraits of Confucius, H. H. Kung and Mussolini, a Sun Yat-sen memorial model and trophies for the Canidrome and the Shanghai Cricket Club. It also shows a sitter beside her sculpted likeness.
