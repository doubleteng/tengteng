New Material and Method is a University of Pennsylvania seminar developed by Laia Mogas-Soldevila. As a teaching fellow, I delivered lectures, led discussion sessions, and mentored graduate students through project ideation and production.

The teaching work connects material inquiry with the practical decisions required to develop and fabricate a prototype.
