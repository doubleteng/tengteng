S202 · 上海《申報》 · 1934-08-09

报道列出拟在沪成立生产美术研究社的发起人，滕白也和南京的滕固分别出现。8月15日是当时拟定的日期；成立大会另见11月11日报道。Teng Baiye and Teng Gu are listed separately among the organisers of a Shanghai society for art and production. August 15 was the proposed date; the founding meeting was reported in November.
