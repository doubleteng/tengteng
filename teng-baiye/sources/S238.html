S238 · 萬青力／人民美術出版社 · 1991；2004-06文集本

万青力转述一位上海友人所言：滕白也在文革中因作指画受批判，被迫从事体力劳动。友人称其间妻子受政治压力与他划清界限，离婚后两人各自谋生。Wan Qingli relayed an unnamed Shanghai friend’s account of pressure on Teng’s wife to dissociate herself politically from him. The couple divorced and made separate livelihoods. The account discussed his forced labour but did not date the divorce.
