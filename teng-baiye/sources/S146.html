Council [election of Fellows]

S146 · Royal Society of Arts, London / Journal of the Royal Society of Arts · 1929-11-22（理事会会议1929-11-11）

11月11日，英国皇家艺术学会理事会选举新会员。11月22日会刊第26页的当选名单将滕圭列作“Teng-Kwei, Cambridge, Mass., U.S.A.”，当时登记地点为美国马萨诸塞州剑桥。The Royal Society of Arts Council elected Teng-Kwei a Fellow on 11 November 1929. The 22 November Journal lists him at Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA, on the second page of the Council report.
