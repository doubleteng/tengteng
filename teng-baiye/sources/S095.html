Mark Tobey and the “Two Powers”

S095 · Julie Badiee / World Order · 1986年春夏号

作者以宗教和艺术交流为背景，记述托贝在西雅图结识 Teng Kuie，并将此与他1934年和陶艺家伯纳德·李奇的东方之行联系起来。Badiee uses the variant Teng Kuie in an account of Tobey’s encounter with Chinese calligraphy, presented alongside the 1934 travel plans of Tobey and ceramicist Bernard Leach.
