S257 · 南京《中央日報》／京市新聞 · 1936-05-13

报道写明5月10日上午9时筹备委员会在考试院开会，五人的模型匿名编号参审，10时开始第三次审查。第5号滕白也当选，丁号王如玖备取，备取者获600元津贴再放大模型。像基方案仍交林主席决定。The report places the committee meeting at the Examination Yuan at 9 a.m. on May 10, followed by the third jury at 10. Models by five sculptors were presented under codes. Teng’s no. 5 was selected, Wang Rujiu was named alternate with a 600-yuan allowance, and the base remained for Chairman Lin to decide.
