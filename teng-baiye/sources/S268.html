The Handbook of the Chinese Students in the U.S.A. (1928)

S268 · Chinese Students Alliance in the U.S.A. · 1928

手册印页47的Lily Ho学音乐，地址在洛杉矶，下一行Lin Ho的地址才在欧伯林。八张原页共核十条具名欧伯林地址记录。Ten named entries in the 1928 handbook carry an Oberlin address. On printed page 47 Lily Ho, a music student in Los Angeles, is listed separately from Lin Ho in Oberlin. The 1934 volume is partially searchable and was not used to assign additional names.
