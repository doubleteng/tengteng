I initiated Digital Practice in 2016 as a collaboration between the University of Washington’s Department of Architecture and the Shanghai Institute of Visual Art. The ten-day summer workshop took place on the SIVA campus, with collaborating faculty Brian R. Johnson, Kimo Griggs, and Weida Huang.

The teaching focused on design communication as well as design generation: how teams carry information from a shared digital model through fabrication and assembly. Case studies in Shanghai connected students’ spatial ideas to questions of use, material, and construction.

Teams developed and built three full-scale pavilions. The Shanghai Museum of Trade subsequently acquired all three for public display.
