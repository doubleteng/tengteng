S177 · The Art Digest · 1929-10-01

纽约展讯将滕圭写作 Kwei Teng，预告 Weyhe 画廊10月14日至26日展出他的绘画和水彩，同栏分列随后举行的 Louis Lozowick 展。The calendar lists paintings and watercolours by Kwei Teng at the Weyhe Gallery on 14–26 October, before an exhibition by Louis Lozowick.
