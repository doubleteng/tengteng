[Review of Kwei Teng’s finger paintings at the Weyhe Gallery]

S100 · New York Evening Post · 1929-10-19

评论将滕的指画与书法、手指运笔及日本狩野派比较。作者肯定个别作品，也批评部分画过于着眼技法，留下与宣传稿不同的同期评价。The critic considered the works in relation to calligraphy and the Kanō school, praising the fluency of some pictures while questioning others’ emphasis on technique.
