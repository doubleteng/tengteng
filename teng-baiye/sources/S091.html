Oral history interview with Andrew Chinn, 1991 August 9

S091 · Andrew Chinn；Matthew Kangas访谈 / Smithsonian Archives of American Art · 1991-08-09

访谈者在讨论托贝绘画线条时提及 Teng Kuei，并对流传的学书说法表示不确定。Chinn质疑被讨论的笔触是否属于中国笔法。此为后世艺术家对作品的解读。Interviewer Matthew Kangas names Teng Kuei during a discussion of Tobey’s brushwork. Chinn questions whether the stroke looks Chinese. Their exchange documents a later interpretation, rather than firsthand testimony about the two artists’ meeting.
