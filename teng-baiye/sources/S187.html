S187 · 瘂弦（书面访问）；李金髮（答） · 2007-09-30（《文學研究》刊本）；通讯始于1974年后

入围者李金髮回忆，他在南京寓所塑孙中山半身像，为大型铜像作准备，历时约两年。他说头奖归于他人，自己赴广州后这尊模型无人保存。这段答话没有点名获选者。Finalist Li Jinfa recalled working for about two years on a Sun Yat-sen bust at his Nanjing residence as preparation for a larger bronze. He said someone else won and his own model went unpreserved after he left for Guangzhou. His reply does not identify the winner.
