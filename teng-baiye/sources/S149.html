S149 · 滕白也 / 上海中華書局《新中華》 · 1935-06-25

文章从自然、个人想象和前人积累的智慧谈美术的来源，并在结尾讨论中国艺术家如何取材于自己的社会生活。印页74标明续接84页。Teng discussed nature, imagination and the accumulated wisdom of past artists as sources of art. His conclusion asks Chinese artists to draw on the life around them. The conclusion is printed ten pages after the opening.
