Mark Tobey papers — Inventory, Accession 3593-002

S090 · University of Washington Libraries, Special Collections · 日记1934；清册未署刊年

托贝档案把1934年上海日记列在第7箱9至22夹，共149页，并将香港、赴日航程及京都的日记另列。清册可定位手稿，未提供日记内容。The inventory locates 149 pages of Shanghai journals from 1934 in box 7, folders 9–22. Hong Kong, the voyage to Japan and Kyoto are separately listed. The inventory does not reproduce the diary.
