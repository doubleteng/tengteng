S169 · Yenching University, President’s Office · 1933-06-10

艺术社预告滕先生画展于6月11至12日在校画廊向公众开放，随后同展拟于6月14至21日在北平中央公园展出。校内展的开放时间为上午9时至晚7时。The Art Club announced a public exhibition of Mr. T’eng’s paintings at the university gallery on 11–12 June, from 9 a.m. to 7 p.m., and a second showing at Central Park, Peiping, on 14–21 June.
