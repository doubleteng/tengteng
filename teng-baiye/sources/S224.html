S224 · 李金髮，《蕉風》 · 1965-09，第155期

李金髮在马来西亚刊出的自传回忆，南京为拟立在新街口的孙中山像征集雕塑，五位复选者各得津贴七百元，再工作数月。李自述花约两年，制作的是二尺高的头像，原拟随大像的尺寸放大；评选后没有取得头奖。其余有关评审动机的文字是他的个人判断。In his serialized memoir, finalist Li Jinfa recalled that five sculptors each received a 700-yuan allowance to continue work for several months on the Xinjiekou commission. He described spending roughly two years on a two-chi portrait head intended for enlargement. He did not receive the top award. His comments about the judges’ motives are personal opinions.
