美国陆军档案分类法：文职雇员类别War Department Decimal File System: civilian employee classifications

S340 · U.S. War Department / NARA · 1943

分类法印页39—40列201为个人记录、230为文职雇员、230.01为职称、230.02为申请与推荐，另列雇用合同和当地雇工等子类。它解释了工程处第158箱与贵阳第338箱的200类文件为何与任职问题相关。Printed pp. 39–40 assign code 201 to individual records, 230 to civilian employees, 230.01 to job titles and 230.02 to applications and recommendations, with further employment-contract and local-labor categories. These codes explain the relevance of Engineer Section box 158 and Kweiyang box 338.
