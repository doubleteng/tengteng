S322 · 刊名未见；观止 · 刊期未详

观止在“短闻”栏中讥称，滕白也掌握了替要人塑像的“交际秘法”：主动承塑，却宣称应孙科之邀。文中点到于右任、唐绍仪及尤列，又以“鲁迅死，你敢塑像否”的反问，影射艺术家对酬劳与政治人物的选择。这段文字集中表现小报对雕塑委托和社会交游的讽刺，动机指控属于作者意见。In the “Short News” column, Guanzhi mocks Teng’s supposed “secret of social success”: voluntarily sculpting prominent men while presenting the work as commissioned by Sun Ke. The item names Yu Youren, Tang Shaoyi and You Lie, then asks whether he would sculpt the recently deceased Lu Xun. It satirizes the relationship between portrait commissions, money and political connections; its account of Teng’s motives remains the columnist’s judgment.
