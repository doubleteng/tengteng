This schematic design study examines tower façades for the Jinji Lake Land 512 development in Suzhou. I developed and compared elevation options and used environmental modeling to assess the site conditions that informed the design.

My contribution during July–September 2011 connected façade studies, parametric modeling, and environmental analysis with the project team’s architectural decisions.
