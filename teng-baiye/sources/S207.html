S207 · 上海《申報》 · 1934-11-16

滕白也致函潘洪生治丧处，称拟将肖像赠给家属。报道说雕塑已经开始，预计约一个月完工；未见落成或交付记录。A news item reported that Teng had begun a memorial portrait of Pan Hongsheng, intended as a gift to the family through the funeral committee. Completion was projected a month later.
