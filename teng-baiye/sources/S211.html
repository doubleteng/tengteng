S211 · 上海《申報》 · 1935-09-18

筹备国乐公演的报道列李廷松为会长、滕白也为总干事。次月滕白也致报馆的信进一步说明自己承担的是义务筹备工作。The report names Li Tingsong as president and Teng as secretary-general while the Chinese music ensemble organised its concert. Teng clarified the voluntary nature of his role in a later letter.
