指画巨擘滕白也

2016年重编本；原作初刊未核 · 郑逸梅；《前尘旧梦》北方文艺版，QQ阅读

郑逸梅记滕白也晚年某夏跌伤胫骨、此后卧床，并在文革叙述中提及因绘画和海外交往遭批判、强制劳动及夫妻离婚。文章未给离婚日期，也没有交代妻子此后的职业或住处。Zheng recalls a fall in summer that injured Teng’s shin and left him bedridden. He also describes political attacks over painting and foreign contacts, compulsory labour and divorce. The text supplies no divorce date or details of the wife’s subsequent work or residence.

晚年记述与出处The later-life account and its source

所读为QQ阅读公开展示的《前尘旧梦》第25章。书页标北方文艺出版社，2016年8月上架，署授权发行。这里保存释述与版本说明，未重印整篇。The text is publicly displayed as chapter 25 of the authorized QQ Reading edition of Qianchen jiumeng, listed by Northern Literature and Art Publishing House in August 2016. This page preserves a research summary rather than a full reproduction.

文中的强制劳动及离婚叙述，与万青力文集第230页相近；目前不能证明两个记述来自独立消息。郑文只称夫人，未具名；没有离婚日期。开篇的去年和冬季也不能由2016年的上架日期反推。Its account of labour and divorce resembles Wan Qingli’s p. 230, without proof of independent sources. Zheng does not name the wife or date the divorce. The relative dates cannot be resolved using the 2016 listing date.
