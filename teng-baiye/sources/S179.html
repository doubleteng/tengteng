The Fifty-Sixth St. Galleries — Teng-Kwei: Modern Chinese Paintings

S179 · The Art Digest（画廊广告） · 1930-01-01

画廊自刊广告将滕圭的展品列为中国现代绘画，同栏逐人区分其他参展者的雕塑与绘画，并列出1月11日闭展。The gallery’s advertisement identifies Teng-Kwei’s contribution as modern Chinese paintings. It distinguishes his work from sculpture elsewhere in the show and gives 11 January as the closing date.
