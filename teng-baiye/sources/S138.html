Annual Catalogue of the University of Washington for 1928

S138 · University of Washington / University of Washington Press · 1928

1927年授位名册在 Bachelor of Fine Arts 栏列 Dun, Kwei。1927–28学生名册另列他为 Grad，China。后者证明曾作研究生注册，单凭该栏不能断定已获硕士。The 1927 degree list names Dun, Kwei under Bachelor of Fine Arts. The 1927–28 student register separately lists him as Grad, from China. Graduate registration alone does not establish the award of a master’s degree.
