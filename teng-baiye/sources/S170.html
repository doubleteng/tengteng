List of Material Submitted to Ministry of Education, Nanking, for Chicago Exhibition

S170 · Yenching University, Peiping；United Board archives · 1933年1月（纽约办公室收件批注）

清单在 Harvard-Yenching Institute 栏记滕圭指画两件，未列题名；另列汉砖拓片四张。首页手注纽约办公室1933年1月25日收。同卷5月函询中方参加芝加哥博览会的安排，不能据送审清单认定作品最终展出。The submission list for the Chicago exposition records two untitled finger drawings by Teng Kuei under the Harvard-Yenching Institute. A handwritten receipt dates it to January 1933. A May letter in the same file asks about exhibition arrangements, so the checklist does not establish display.
