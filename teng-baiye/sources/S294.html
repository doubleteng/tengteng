欧伯林与侯涵辉Oberlin and Hou Hanhui

Alumni Records (RG 28)

馆藏1833–2024；指南起草1991 · Oberlin College Archives · Brian Williams

校友档案包括幼师学校学生。第II系列收1966届及以前毕业生，以及该范围中1966年7月1日后去世的未毕业生；可能保存地址变更、校友调查表、剪报及讣告。The alumni files include the training school. Series II covers graduates through 1966 and non-graduates in that range who died after July 1, 1966. Files may contain questionnaires, address changes and obituaries.

馆方曾在1916、1926、1936、1948年寄发传记调查表；1960年校友名录覆盖已知毕业生和未毕业生。馆藏标示Unrestricted，但未公开逐人文件。不能据此认定侯涵辉填过表，或把可能内容写成她的经历。Biographical forms were sent in 1916, 1926, 1936 and 1948. The 1960 register covered known graduates and former students. The collection is unrestricted, but individual files are not displayed online; no Hou questionnaire has been found.

校友调查表可能帮助核对出生、就读年限、家庭及职业；地址变更、剪报和讣告可能连接归国后的生活。这里列的是馆藏可能包含的材料，不能写成侯涵辉已经证实的经历。Questionnaires may identify birth, attendance, family and career; address changes and obituaries may connect later life. These are possible contents of the collection, not established events in Hou’s life.

1960年《Alumni register: graduates and former students, teaching and administrative staff, 1833–1960》可作为姓名索引，馆藏号LD4179.A16。已找到的在线副本标示受限，本次未获得可检索全文。The 1960 Alumni register, shelfmark LD4179.A16, is another name-index lead. The located online copy is access-restricted; its full text was not searched.
