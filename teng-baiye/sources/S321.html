滕白也乳名小狗滕白也乳名小狗

S321 · 刊名未见；署名两字，影印不清 · 刊期未详

这则短文从高桥乡里与早年家境谈起，将滕白也赴海外学习、从事雕塑和指画的经历，与“小狗”这一乳名串联起来。作者把乡间旧称和成名后的艺术家身份并置，既谈他的技艺，也夹杂对性情及作品的揶揄。This short piece takes the childhood nickname “Little Dog” as its theme, digresses on Gaoqiao place names, and discusses Teng’s sculpture, finger painting, reputation and temperament. Its account of artistic ability mixes with teasing commentary, placing a village nickname alongside the artist’s public identity. The nickname, local details and judgments of his work are presented as this newspaper’s account.
