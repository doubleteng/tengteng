S123 · 滕白也 / 廣西省佛教會《獅子吼月刊》 · 1941-12-15

诗以桂林小筑为题，题赠巨赞法师，并与同版其他作者的诗分列。原刊著录作者为滕白也。A poem addressed to the Buddhist monk Juzan evokes a small dwelling in Guilin. The printed page identifies Teng Baiye separately from neighboring contributors.
