Correspondence with Carl Zigrosser, 1931–1932, n.d.

S162 · Elva Keith／University of Pennsylvania, Kislak Center · 1931–1932及未标日期

纽约书商Harry F. Marks的同事 Elva Keith 在信中谈她与Zigrosser共同的朋友滕圭及版画。全组4件，其中一封有插图，插图内容及作者未核。Keith, associated with Manhattan bookseller Harry F. Marks, wrote about her and Zigrosser’s mutual friend Kuei T’eng and about prints. Four items are catalogued, including an illustrated letter; the illustration’s subject is not specified.
