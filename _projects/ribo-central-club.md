RIBO Central Club is a 300-square-meter project at the RIBO group headquarters in Shanghai. The program includes dining, a bar, reception, and a reading area.

Working with Shaojie Yan, I developed the project through schematic design, design development, and construction drawings. The design work took place during 2009–2010, and the detailed portfolio account records completion in 2012.
