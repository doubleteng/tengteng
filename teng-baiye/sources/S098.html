Kwei to Speak

S098 · The Harvard Crimson · 1931-02-25

预告滕圭在 Evangeline Walker 位于 Holyoke Street 2号的画室主讲 The Background of Chinese Art。它是六场沙龙中的首场，茶会为旱灾募款。An advance notice names Teng as the first speaker in a series of six studio salons at Evangeline Walker’s studio, 2 Holyoke Street. His subject was The Background of Chinese Art. The associated tea supported drought relief.
