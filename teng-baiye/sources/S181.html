Four Nations Share Prizes at Woodcut and Lithograph Annual

S181 · The Art Digest · 1931-12-15

芝加哥艺术博物馆第三届国际石版画与木刻展的录选名单在中国项下只列 Teng Kwei。文章说明展至1月24日，未列他的作品题名。The list of artists accepted for the Art Institute of Chicago’s third international lithograph and wood-engraving exhibition names Teng Kwei alone under China. The article gives a closing date of 24 January without naming his print.
