滕白也 · Teng Baiye

《工兵部队：对日战争》中的贵阳工程活动The Corps of Engineers: The War Against Japan, pp. 662–663

S329 · Karl C. Dod / U.S. Army Center of Military History · 1966

美国陆军战史记1945年4月下旬，魏德迈令滇缅公路工程人员协助中国陆军工兵整修安顺至贵阳的道路；同年6月，中国战区后勤部又将第1880航空工兵营派往昆明至芷江道路工程。The official Army history records Wedemeyer directing Burma Road engineers in late April 1945 to assist Chinese Army engineers on the Anshun–Kweiyang road. In June, Services of Supply detailed the 1880th Engineer Aviation Battalion to the Kunming–Chihchiang road.
