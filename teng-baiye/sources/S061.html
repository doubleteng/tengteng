Finger Tip Paintings by Kwei Dun / Finger-Tip Painting and the Painter

S061 · The Town Crier；访谈署 A. M. B.；Seattle Public Library · 1928-12-15，卷23期50，页40–41

第40页刊五幅署名指画，第41页刊本人肖像、作画访谈与浮雕 Machinery。图书馆记录这期为圣诞号。The Christmas issue reproduces five titled finger paintings on p. 40. Page 41 includes a portrait, an interview and the bas-relief Machinery. Seattle Public Library dates the issue 15 December 1928.
