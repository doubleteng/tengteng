Correspondence with Carl Zigrosser, 1929–1964

S163 · Helen Lacey Haskell／University of Pennsylvania, Kislak Center · 1929–1964

海伦·莱西·哈斯克尔致Zigrosser的通信涉及两人共同的艺术家朋友滕圭，并谈Treetops营地与亲友往来。目录未指明哪封写到滕圭。Penn identifies Kuei T’eng as a mutual friend discussed in Helen Lacey Haskell’s correspondence with Zigrosser. The finding aid does not assign the reference to a particular letter.
