S016 · 奉贤文旅云 · 网页未署日期

奉贤区人物条目记滕白也在上海卫生学校模型室任技师，加入农工党、民盟、上海市美术家协会，并当选徐汇区政协委员。地方条目将落实政策平反记在1978年12月，继记受聘上海文史馆、两年后病逝。市文史馆名录将平反记为1979年。两条记载应并列。The district directory records employment as a model-room technician at Shanghai Health School and election to the Xuhui District political consultative committee. It dates rehabilitation to December 1978 and reports his appointment to the municipal institute and death two years later. The institute’s own roster dates rehabilitation to 1979.
