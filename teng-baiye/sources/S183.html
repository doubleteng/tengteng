S183 · The China Weekly Review · 1934-05-05

上海英文周刊在讲演后两日记载，滕圭于5月3日向皇家亚洲文会北中国支会讲述竹与竹画，并使用幻灯片。Two days after the event, the Shanghai weekly records Teng Kuei’s 3 May lecture on bamboo painting before the North China Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, illustrated with lantern slides.
