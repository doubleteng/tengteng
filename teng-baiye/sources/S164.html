Correspondence with Carl Zigrosser, 1927–1971, n.d.

S164 · Jessie Warneke；著录主款目 Heinz (Heinrich) Warneke／University of Pennsylvania, Kislak Center · 1927–1971及未标日期

杰西·瓦内克的通信提及滕圭，记录1930年代旅欧艺术家及纽约友人。宾大滕氏通信目录另记他在法国借住她与雕塑家Heinz Warneke的工作室。Although the catalogue is filed under sculptor Heinz Warneke, Penn identifies Jessie Warneke as the letter writer. Kuei T’eng is named among her correspondents’ circle; the separate Teng file describes a stay at the couple’s studio in France.
