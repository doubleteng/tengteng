Try one change

  1. Change a load

    Use the default Howe · 10 panels model. In Design, click the central downward load arrow (node 16), then choose Up in its Direction controls.

  2. Read the response

    Open Analyze. Compare tension, compression, and Peak movement. Switch the same load between Down and Up to see the difference.

  3. Inspect the path

    Open Toolpath and keep Axial + bending stress. After generation finishes, compare member fill and tensile runs at the central joints. Return to Design to repeat.

Keep geometry and other settings unchanged. Path continuity is a geometric check; the prototype does not verify printed joint strength.
