Letter from the Harvard University Bursar to B. A. Garside concerning Teng Kwei’s fellowship balance

S172 · Harvard University, Bursar’s Office / B. A. Garside · 1932-04-29

哈佛财务官通知燕京大学财务主任，附寄450美元支票作为滕圭研究员津贴余款，并明确写道滕圭当时已经在燕京大学，要求转交。金额是余额，不是资助总额。The Harvard bursar sent Garside a $450 cheque to forward to Teng Kwei at Yenching University, identifying it as the remaining fellowship balance. The letter explicitly locates Teng at Yenching by 29 April 1932.
