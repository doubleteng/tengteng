文章开头引托贝第一人称回忆，明确以 Teng Kuei 为教授其书法方法的中国画家。注1将这段话定位到1966年汉诺威 Kestner-Gesellschaft 展览图录第27页。The opening reproduces Tobey’s own statement identifying Teng Kuei as the Chinese painter with whom he studied calligraphic method. Note 1 traces it to the 1966 Hannover catalogue, p. 27.
