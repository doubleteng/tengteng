微風文藝社播音演講

S205 · 上海《申報》 · 1934-10-03

报上预告当日下午四时，微风文艺社将在四马路中西药房播音台安排滕白也主讲中国美术思潮。The society’s radio announcement scheduled Teng to speak on currents in Chinese art at 4 p.m., from the Zhongxi Pharmacy station on Fourth Road in Shanghai.
