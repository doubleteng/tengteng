S120 · 滕白也 / 藝風雜誌社 · 1935-05-16

作者梳理指画史上的署名画家，论及唐代记载、清人指画和当时的从业人数。页上的人形剪影未署名。Teng traces earlier accounts of finger painting from the Tang through the Qing and comments on the small number of contemporary practitioners. The silhouetted illustration on the page is unsigned.
