Mary Yung-Foh Hu / Mary Hu · 1923、1927

清华校史馆对名胡永馥。黄自子女记她在上海去世，早于侯涵辉1934年的婚礼。A Tsinghua manifest study names Hu. Huang Tzu’s children recalled that she died in Shanghai before Hou Hanhui married in 1934.
