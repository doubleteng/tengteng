藝風社第二屆展覽會出品目錄

S127 · 藝風社 / 《藝風》 · 1935-05-16

滕白也栏列第17至21号展品。第19号《長松》、第21号《三鵝》可辨。同刊图版印有《三鵝》的题名和“滕白也指畫”署名。第18、20号尚有字待辨。The exhibition list groups entries 17–21 under Teng. No. 19 is Tall Pine and no. 21 is Three Geese. A plate in the same issue credits the latter to Teng as a finger painting. Two titles remain partly illegible.
