S212 · 滕白也 / 《申報》 · 1935-10-13

滕白也在10月11日署名致主笔，订正10月9日的演出报道。他说明演出是为乐团十周年筹办，自己担任义务干事、忙于筹划，并否认指挥排练。In a letter signed October 11, Teng corrected the newspaper’s October 9 concert report. He described himself as a volunteer organiser of the ensemble’s tenth-anniversary concert and denied directing its rehearsals.
