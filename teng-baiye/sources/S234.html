Chinesische Malerei der Gegenwart – Der Teufelsvertreiber Chung K’uei von T’eng Po-yeh

S234 · Kunstgewerbemuseum Zürich；展览导览第152册 · 1942-05-10—31

图录以 T’eng Po-yeh 署名，称此幅《钟馗》为指画，藏主为陶德曼博士（Oskar P. Trautmann）。画中钟馗转身回望，画幅右边有长题款。与1934年柏林图版174相比，面部、足部、墨色和题款位置均不同，是另一幅《钟馗》。The catalogue names T’eng Po-yeh as the artist, identifies the work as a finger painting, and names Dr Oskar P. Trautmann as its owner. The backward-looking figure, feet, ink handling and long right-hand inscription distinguish it from the 1934 Berlin catalogue’s plate 174.
