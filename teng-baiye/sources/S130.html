Bamboo Symbol in Chinese Art: Significance and History Described

S130 · The North-China Herald · 1934-05-09

报道滕圭向亚洲文会会员及来宾作画竹讲演，比较唐、元、清的画竹传统，并记述他怎样用墨的浓淡表现竹子的向背、风雨与距离。The report of Teng’s address to the Royal Asiatic Society discusses bamboo painting from the Tang onward, the use of ink values, and the conditions a painter observes in depicting bamboo. It is a contemporary report of the lecture.
