Mark Tobey: TOBEY or not to be ? — dossier de presse

S114 · Galerie Jeanne Bucher Jaeger / Collection de Bueil & Ract-Madoux, Paris · 2020–2021

巴黎展览的法文资料第5页将滕具名为 Teng K’wei，记载他教托贝书法。该资料采用1922年相识的说法，不能据此改写入境年表。The French press dossier identifies Teng K’wei as Tobey’s calligraphy teacher and dates their friendship to 1922. This later chronology is not independent evidence for Teng’s arrival date.
