At the Shanghai Institute of Visual Art, I supervised undergraduate theses spanning retail environments, workplace renovation, campus sports facilities, and spatial interaction. Across architecture and product design, this teaching included more than twenty theses during 2015–2017.

Yanyu Chen’s *Design and Renovation of a Lifestyle Retail Street* explores the spatial organization of commercial activity. Chuou Zhang’s *Interactive Renovation Design of Workplace* uses post-occupancy evaluation to identify concerns about daylight, shared space, noise, and privacy. Zhang’s proposal develops movable partitions that change the relationship between circulation, conversation, and rest.

Lu Ye’s *Campus Stadium Renovation Design* integrates sports facilities with structural, daylight, and facade studies. *The Flowing Space* explores how a transformable partition changes an interior, using perspectives, configuration studies, and a project film.

The projects shown are student work developed under my supervision.
