据吴霖所引1934年《申报》，滕白也是上海生产美术研究社发起人之一，并与宋维赓负责第四队征求会员。8月15日是筹备时宣布的拟议日期，吴霖另记11月10日开成立大会。Wu Lin cites an August 1934 Shenbao notice naming Teng among the organizers of a Shanghai applied-arts society. Teng and Song Weigeng led its fourth recruitment team. Wu distinguishes the proposed August opening from the November inaugural meeting.
