Envoy Airs Views on War in Chinese Room Dedication

S229 · The Pittsburgh Press · 1939-10-07

该报在中国教室落成典礼次日刊登中国驻美大使胡适的肖像及谈话，写明胡适到匹兹堡参加献室典礼，并报道他在典礼上的致辞。照片中的人物为胡适。A report published the day after the Chinese Room dedication describes Ambassador Hu Shih’s visit and address in Pittsburgh, with a captioned portrait. It documents the completed room’s public dedication, without identifying the architect.
