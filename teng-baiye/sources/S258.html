S258 · 才陵／南京《中央日報》本報特寫 · 1936-05-20

滕白也说自己来自上海浦东奉贤县，幼时在上海求学；在国外研究雕刻绘画约十年，回国后在燕京大学授课，民国二十二年秋回上海开馆。他说曾搜集五十余张照片、制作七具头像供比较；预计先在南京制作石膏肖像，再运上海翻砂。估计工期约一年，打算把合同期限写为两年。报纸配刊滕近影、孙中山头像及模型全景。Teng placed his hometown in Fengxian, Pudong, and described schooling in Shanghai, study abroad, teaching at Yenching, and his return to Shanghai in autumn 1933 to open a studio. For the Sun Yat-sen project he assembled more than fifty photographs and made seven trial heads. He proposed making the plaster in Nanjing and having it cast in Shanghai, allowing one year for work and two in a prospective contract. Three photographs accompany the interview.
