滕白也指畫潤例Teng Baiye’s rates for finger paintings

1924-06-16 · 《申报》

第8版左侧刊指画润例。堂幅三尺八元、四尺十二元、五尺十六元、六尺二十四元、八尺四十元、丈匹六十元。In a narrow column on page 8, Teng offers hanging paintings in six sizes, from eight yuan for three chi to sixty yuan for a zhang-sized sheet.
