Det universella språket: Efterkrigstidens abstrakta konst / The Universal Language: Post-War Abstract Art

S111 · Kristoffer Arvidsson / Göteborgs konstmuseum · 2018

哥德堡美术馆在托贝《构图》（1957，GKM 1695）旁，以瑞典文和英文介绍托贝认识中国画家滕圭后对书法发生兴趣。图录将会面写为1922年，与入境资料年份相异。A bilingual entry alongside Tobey’s Composition (1957, GKM 1695) identifies Teng Kuei as a Chinese painter who stimulated his interest in calligraphy. Its 1922 meeting date differs from the documented arrival chronology.
