This research brings together concrete material development, additive manufacturing, and geometric design to investigate carbon capture and storage. Printed triply periodic minimum surfaces form part of the architectural material studies.

The associated collaborative paper, *3D Concrete Printing of Triply Periodic Minimum Surfaces for Enhanced Carbon Capture and Storage*, was published in *Advanced Functional Materials* in 2025.
