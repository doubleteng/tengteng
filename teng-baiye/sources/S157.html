A Finding Aid to the Carl Zigrosser Papers, 1891–1971

访问原网页 ↗Visit original website ↗

S157 · Archives of American Art, Smithsonian Institution · 档案覆盖1891–1971，非滕氏信件起讫

档案馆在 Zigrosser 的具名通信者中列 Kuei Teng。馆方保存缩微副本，原件藏宾夕法尼亚大学。Zigrosser 曾服务于纽约Weyhe画廊及费城艺术博物馆。Kuei Teng is specifically named among Zigrosser’s correspondents. The Smithsonian holds microfilm, and locates the original papers at the University of Pennsylvania. Zigrosser worked at the Weyhe Gallery and the Philadelphia Museum of Art.
