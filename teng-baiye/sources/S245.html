全國美展今日開幕

S245 · 上海《申報》 · 1937-04-01

南京全国美展正式公开前一日的会场记事中，滕白也列入到场者名单。可与两件雕塑及指画屏的同期展品目录分开互证。The Nanjing exhibition’s opening report names Teng among those at the venue before its public opening. This independently complements the catalogue entries for his sculptures and finger painting.
