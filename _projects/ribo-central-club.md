  heading: A shared ground within the campus
  body: The site sits where the headquarters’ research and design, production, and residential areas meet. Existing
    pedestrian routes cross the limited space beside the brick buildings. Lifting part of the clubhouse preserves
    a passage and creates a sheltered terrace below, while the volume bends around a small courtyard. The building

    the interior, courtyard, and surrounding paths.

  body: 'Reception, dining, a bar, and a reading area occupy a connected sequence of changing levels. Broad timber
    steps bridge the height difference and provide places to sit, turning circulation into an extension of the lounge.
    The plan wraps these activities around the courtyard, so moving through the building continually changes the
    relationship between the interior and the outdoor terrace.


    Floor-to-ceiling glazing keeps the compact spaces visually connected. Along the raised reading area, a full-height
    shelving wall gives the interior a distinct edge behind the glass; across the courtyard, the projecting upper
    room frames a more enclosed space above the entrance.'

RIBO Central Club is a **300 m² clubhouse at the RIBO Group headquarters in Qingpu District, Shanghai**. Completed in 2012, it was my first built project. Designed for employees and visiting clients, it brings dining, reading, meetings, and informal gathering into a small addition to the existing campus.
