FRUS编者注记，时任燕京大学校长的司徒雷登在1941—1945年被日军拘押于北平。这使“经司徒雷登介绍”的具体时间和传递方式成为1945年贵阳任职记载中的独立问题。An FRUS editorial note states that Stuart, then president of Yenching University, was held in Japanese military custody in Peiping from 1941 to 1945. The timing and transmission of his reported recommendation therefore require separate examination.
