An Index to Reproductions of Paintings by Twentieth-Century Chinese Artists: Revised Edition

S094 · Ellen Johnston Laing / University of Michigan Center for Chinese Studies · 1998（修订版）

滕圭条目列有指画 Lotus and duck，有题字及署名，并指向南京展览图录第二册第543号。该题录提供战时桂林、重庆、成都的活动线索。作品是否与2021年拍品相同仍待比图。The entry for T’eng Kuei (Pai-yeh) indexes a signed, inscribed finger painting, Lotus and duck, to the Nanking exhibition catalogue, II.543. It also lists wartime locations. No physical identity with the 2021 auction lot has been established.
