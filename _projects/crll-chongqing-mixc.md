This façade study for Chongqing MIXC draws on the city’s mountainous terrain and its representation in Chinese landscape painting. Digital modeling translates the image-based composition into a system of pixelated façade components.

I developed the façade design in Callison’s Shanghai office in 2011, working with Nu Guo and Shark Liang.
