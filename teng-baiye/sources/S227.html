S227 · The Art News · 1929-06-15

报道说布鲁克林博物馆6月1日开幕的国际群展计划展至10月1日，名单中列 Kwei Teng，国别为中国。The report names Kwei Teng of China among the artists in the Brooklyn Museum exhibition that opened on 1 June and was scheduled to remain on view until 1 October.
