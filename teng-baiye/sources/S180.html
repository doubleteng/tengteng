S180 · The Art News · 1930-01-04

展评写滕圭用乌贼墨色的水墨表现山水、鸭、竹和瀑布，尤其欣赏一幅竹枝小品用笔节制，同时对他与古代画家的差距提出批评。原文没有给这些作品逐件定名。The reviewer describes Teng’s sepia ink-and-brush landscapes, ducks, bamboo and waterfalls. The writer singles out a small bamboo painting for its economy but also judges his brushwork against earlier Chinese masters. No catalogue titles are given.
