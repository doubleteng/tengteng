Le peintre Mark Tobey

S236 · Jacques Michel / Le Monde · 1976-04-27

雅克·米歇尔在托贝去世后的评论中，点名中国书法家 Teng Kwei（滕圭）。他认为滕使托贝得以进入东亚艺术的空间观，画面会随观看者的视点改变。Writing after Tobey’s death, Jacques Michel named Chinese calligrapher Teng Kwei as the person who opened Tobey’s eyes to East Asian art and connected the encounter with a pictorial space that changes with the viewer’s position.
