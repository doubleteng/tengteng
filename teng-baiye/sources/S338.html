司徒雷登驻华大使任命记录John Leighton Stuart: ambassadorial appointment

S338 · U.S. Department of State, Office of the Historian · 1946-07-12

美国国务院记司徒雷登于1946年7月12日获任驻华大使，7月19日递交国书。年表所记1945年贵阳工作发生在任命之前。The State Department dates Stuart’s appointment as ambassador to 12 July 1946 and presentation of credentials to 19 July. Teng’s reported Kweiyang employment in 1945 preceded this appointment.

与生平记载的关系Relation to the biographical record

1945年介绍任职的说法不应冠以“时任美国驻华大使”。此记录仅确定任命时间，不证明或否定推荐本身。Stuart should not be called the serving U.S. ambassador in a 1945 account. This record fixes his appointment date; it neither confirms nor disproves the recommendation.
