S102 · The Stockbridge Brief-Sun / Capital Area District Libraries · 1930-06-26

密歇根小报在报道纽约画展时称滕圭用指尖作画、指甲勾线，并将其技法与指尖触觉联系起来。短讯没有注明画廊或确切展期。A Michigan newspaper item describes Teng applying paint with fingertips and drawing with a nail, linking the method to tactile sensitivity. It mentions New York without naming the gallery or giving exhibition dates.
