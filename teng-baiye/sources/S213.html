第309号《Journey in White》为托贝1956年作品。其旁的传略以 Teng Kwei 具名，记1934年托贝在上海向滕学画。此页提供战后欧洲策展界对师承关系的记录。The entry accompanying Tobey’s 1956 Journey in White, no. 309, identifies Teng Kwei as the artist who taught him in Shanghai in 1934. It documents how the European exhibition literature described their relationship.
