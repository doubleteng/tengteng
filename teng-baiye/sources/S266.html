S266 · Oberlin College students · 1927–1928

1927年版第47页列Mary Yung-Foh Hu，汉口，教育；第168页列Mary Hu为中国学生会主席。1928年版第195页列学院、幼师学校和当地中学的中国学生会会员人数，未指出幼师生姓名。 该卷印页175的社团报道明确把当年写作1926–1927学年。The 1927 yearbook identifies Mary Yung-Foh Hu of Hankow with Education and lists Mary Hu as the club president. The 1928 club page counts students from the college, the kindergarten training school and the local high school without identifying the kindergarten trainee. Page 175 of the 1928 volume explicitly describes the 1926–27 academic year.
