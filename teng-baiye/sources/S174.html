燕京大學教職員學生名錄 / Yenching University Directory, 1932–1933

S174 · 燕京大學 / Yenching University · 1932-11

国文学系正式教员名单中，以中英文并排列“滕圭 / Teng Kwei”及“Lecturer in Fine Arts / 美術講師”。这是校方对其1932–33学年任职的双语记录。The directory restricts this list to regular departmental faculty. Under Chinese it identifies Teng Gui (Teng Kwei) as Lecturer in Fine Arts, with the Chinese rank printed alongside.
