微風文藝社成立

S201 · 上海《申報》 · 1934-07-23

微风文艺社的成立大会于7月22日在上海女中举行。报道留下文艺社人员分工，滕白也名列美术一项。A report of the July 22 founding meeting of the Weifeng literary society at Shanghai Girls’ School names Teng in the division of responsibilities for art.
