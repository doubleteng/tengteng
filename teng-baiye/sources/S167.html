S167 · Yenching University, President’s Office · 1933-02-04

校刊预告美国艺术家 Thomas Handforth 将展出北平生活题材的素描及蚀刻版画，并谈蚀刻技法。拟放映的电影为波士顿美术馆影片的拷贝，约两年前由 Harvard Film Foundation 交给滕先生。An announcement of Thomas Handforth’s etching talk identifies the proposed film as a duplicate from Boston’s Museum of Fine Arts given to Mr. T’eng by the Harvard Film Foundation two years earlier. The notice leaves the event date and location open.
