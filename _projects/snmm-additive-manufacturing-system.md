The Single-Nozzle Multi-Filament (SNMF) system combines multiple filament feeds in an actively mixed extrusion head. Programmable feed ratios create continuous changes in material composition within a single printed object.

A numerical model describes how material mixtures transition inside the nozzle. Experiments connect feed commands to the deposited composition, allowing material interfaces to be placed with greater precision.

Three design methods translate intended material distributions into printable instructions: image sampling, two-dimensional discrete patches, and three-dimensional surface division. Six case studies test the system across different geometries and functional requirements, including structural specimens and objects with graded material properties.
