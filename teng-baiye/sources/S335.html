南京美国陆军顾问团沿革，1946—1948Army Advisory Group China: organizational history, 1946–1948

S335 · U.S. National Archives (NARA) · 1946–1948

南京总部司令部于1946年10月28日改称Army Advisory Group China（美国陆军顾问团）。1948年9月才与其他机构合并为JUSMAG。1947年的机构名称应采用AAG。The Nanking Headquarters Command became Army Advisory Group China on 28 October 1946. It merged with other organizations to form JUSMAG in September 1948. AAG is the appropriate institutional name for 1947.
