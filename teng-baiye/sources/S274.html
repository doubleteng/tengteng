Oberlin Conservatory: Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of School Music

S274 · Oberlin College · 1921–1930

校方分别设置音乐学士和学校音乐学士。1924年毕业典礼将Fong-Tsung Tsu列在后者候选栏。同年校友刊印页29记其获B.S.Mus.。1925卷年鉴另述四年制学校音乐教育课程。The conservatory had distinct degrees in music and school music. Fong-Tsung Tsu appears in the school-music candidate list in 1924 and in that year’s alumni record with a degree. The yearbook describes a four-year teacher-training course.
